# Daily Content Generation Task

Objective:

Create one daily cybersecurity content package for Abhedit.

Steps:

1. Find the most important cybersecurity news from the last 24 hours.
2. Verify source credibility.
3. Include source URL.
4. Include publication date.
5. Generate:

   * 6-slide carousel
   * LinkedIn post
   * Facebook post
   * Instagram caption
   * 20 hashtags
6. Follow AGENTS.md.
7. Follow BRANDING.md.
8. Use only one primary story.
9. Keep slides under 30 words.
10. Explain why the news matters to businesses.
