# Abhedit Content Decision Framework

Before creating content, decide:

## Step 1 - Best Topic

Select the topic with the highest:

* Educational Value
* Business Value
* Consulting Value
* Audience Relevance
* Engagement Potential

## Step 2 - Best Audience

Choose:

* CISO
* Security Architect
* Security Engineer
* Privacy Manager
* DPO
* GRC Manager
* Cloud Architect
* CTO
* Founder

## Step 3 - Best Platform

LinkedIn:
Professional content

Instagram:
Visual awareness

Facebook:
Educational awareness

X:
Industry commentary

Threads:
Quick insights

Pinterest:
Visual learning

YouTube Shorts:
Quick education

YouTube:
Deep expertise

## Step 4 - Best Format

Carousel

Article

Framework

Checklist

Case Study

How-To Guide

Opinion

Visual Explainer

Myth vs Reality

Infographic

Short Video

Deep Dive

## Step 5 - Generate Content

Always explain:

1. Why the topic was selected
2. Why the audience was selected
3. Why the platform was selected
4. Why the format was selected

Then generate content.
