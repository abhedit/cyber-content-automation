Build Trust

Build Authority

Generate Leads

Educate Professionals

Increase Brand Awareness

LinkedIn: Professional Authority

Facebook: Education and Awareness

Pinterest: Infographics and Visual Learning

YouTube Shorts: Quick Educational Tips

YouTube Videos: Deep Dives

## Content Mix

40% Educational

20% Frameworks

15% How-To Guides

10% Opinion

10% Industry Trends

5% Threat Intelligence

## Content Principles

Teach, don't sell.

Explain, don't scare.

Focus on business impact.

Focus on practical implementation.

Focus on decision making.

Focus on governance and strategy.

Avoid becoming a vulnerability news page.

Avoid fear-based content.
