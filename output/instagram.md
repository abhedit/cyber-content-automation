## 🔒 Secure Your AI Future: Understanding AI Supply Chain Risk

Did you know the AI systems you rely on have a "supply chain" just like physical products? Every piece of data, every pre-trained model, and every third-party service introduces potential risks.

Recent incidents highlight that *AI supply chain attacks* are a real and growing threat. This isn't just about protecting your servers; it's about the integrity of your AI from its very foundation.

**What's at stake?**
❌ Data Breaches
❌ Model Poisoning
❌ IP Theft
❌ Regulatory Fines
❌ Reputational Damage

**Key Risk Areas:**
*   Data Sourcing 📊
*   Model & Algorithm Components 🧩
*   Cloud & Infrastructure Services ☁️
*   MLOps Tools 🛠️

**How to Mitigate?**
1.  **VET VENDORS:** Rigorous due diligence for all AI partners.
2.  **SECURE DEVOPS:** Integrate security into your AI development lifecycle.
3.  **MONITOR MODELS:** Continuously check for integrity and bias.
4.  **GOVERN DATA:** Strict controls from source to deployment.
5.  **ESTABLISH POLICIES:** Clear governance for AI risk.
